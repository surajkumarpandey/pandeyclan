1ancestral village — Bairati, Almora

A household in the Kumaon hills

Bairati sits in the Almora district of Uttarakhand, in the Kumaon Himalayas — once the heart of the Katyuri and Chand kingdoms, and for generations, home to this family. This site holds what's known of that lineage: who came from whom, where they scattered to, and what's remembered of the village itself.

It starts as a chart, hand-drawn and photographed. It's meant to grow into something fuller — photos, stories, and the whereabouts of everyone who carries this name today.

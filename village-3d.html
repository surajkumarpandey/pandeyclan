Details about a place: tap its label or pin on the map. A card opens with its coordinates, how to reach it, and buttons for Google Maps and directions.

Jump to a place: use the “Go to” buttons above the map. They move the camera without opening the card.

Routes: the two glowing roads are the two ways up to the family house. Tap “Family house” in the Go-to bar to see it up close, then use the button in its card to walk around it in 3D.

Lost? “Reset view” brings back the whole map.

Hills and slopes come from real elevation data (about 30 m detail, heights exaggerated 1.5× so the slopes read clearly). The ground is satellite imagery. Roads are drawn through the points pinned by the family, so the real roads bend more between those points than shown. The family house is drawn 3× its real size so that it can be seen on a model this large; its front wall is the real photograph.

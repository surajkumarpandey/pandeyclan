Bairati in 3D

Real elevation data for the actual hillside — drag to rotate, two fingers (or right-click) to tilt, scroll or pinch to zoom. The pin marks the family house.

Terrain is real elevation data for this hillside, from open public data — genuinely 3D, not an illustration. The house photo is shown as a marker at its location on that terrain; it isn't a 3D reconstruction of the building itself, since that would need multiple photos and dedicated 3D-modelling software, not something a single photo can give us reliably.

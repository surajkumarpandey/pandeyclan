Folk music

Bedu Pako Baro Masa

The best-known Kumaoni folk song — composed in the early 1950s from an older village tradition, and now the official regimental song of the Kumaon Regiment of the Indian Army. Worth knowing the words to.

Festivals

The Kumaoni festival calendar

Harela (monsoon sowing festival), Uttarayani (Bageshwar's winter trade fair), Kumaoni Holi (a musical, court-tradition version of Holi tracing back to the Chand kings), and the Nanda Devi Raj Jat — a pilgrimage held once every twelve years.

Theatre

Kumaoni Ramlila (Almora style)

A distinct, musical style of Ramlila that developed in Almora, recognised by UNESCO — reportedly the longest continuously running theatrical production in the world, at over 150 years.

Dance

Chholiya, Jhoda, Chanchari

Chholiya — performed with swords and shields, historically at weddings — is considered Uttarakhand's oldest folk dance. Jhoda and Chanchari are circular group dances, often performed at Holi.

How to reach the house

Two roads lead from Dwarahat to Bairati. Both pass the village school, and from there the same local road climbs to the house.

Route 1 — along the highway

  1. From Dwarahat, take NH 109 (the Dwarahat–Chaukhutia road) north towards Chaukhutia.
  2. Pass the villages of Bijepur, Suraikhet, Pan and Selagarhi, all along the highway.
  3. Just after Green Valley Restaurant, turn uphill at the Bairati turn-off.
  4. Follow the village road up through the meadows to Govt. Primary School, Bhairati.
  5. About 95 m past the school, take the local road uphill into the village. It leads to the house (about 380 m).

Route 2 — the school road

  1. In Dwarahat, take the road that branches off NH 109 opposite Himalayan Homestay Dwarahat.
  2. It runs above the highway, past the same villages (Bijepur, Suraikhet, Pan and Selagarhi), for about 4 km.
  3. It goes straight past the front of Govt. Primary School, Bhairati. It can also be joined from the north when coming from the Chitreshwar side.
  4. About 95 m past the school, take the local road uphill into the village. It leads to the house (about 380 m).
